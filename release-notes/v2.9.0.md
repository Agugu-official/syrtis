## Features

- **The menu bar gauge names the length of the window it follows.** [#511](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/511), [#512](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/512)

  Gauge icons now lead with a letter for the tracked quota window: H for 6 hours or less (such as the 5-hour window), D for about a day, W for about a week, M for about a month. The letter comes from the window's own length, so it follows the quota source you picked; with Auto it follows whichever window has the least left. There is no letter while a window's length is still unknown, or before there is a reading.

  To turn it off, use "Show window length letter" in the "Menubar icon" section of Settings (shown when a gauge icon is selected). It is on by default. The animated cat, parrot and sand icons are unchanged.
